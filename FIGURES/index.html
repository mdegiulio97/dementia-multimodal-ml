    Figure index

    Every figure in the repository, what it shows and what to take from it. Figures under figures/synthetic/ come from the synthetic ADNIMERGE-shaped cohort (a method check with a known answer, not a clinical result). Figures under figures/open/ come from real open data. ADNI figures are produced locally in figures/adni/ and are not committed.

    MCI-to-dementia conversion (synthetic cohort)

    figures/synthetic/conversion_cindex_forest.png

    Harrell's C-index of the four survival models, computed on out-of-fold predictions (5-fold cross-validation split by participant) with 95% bootstrap intervals. The baseline uses age, MMSE and APOE4 only. In the synthetic cohort the true hazard depends on APOE4, MMSE, ADAS13 and ICV-adjusted hippocampal volume, so the full Cox model should beat the baseline and the flexible models should not do better than the correctly specified Cox model. Both happen (0.72 vs 0.64), which is the check the figure exists for.

    figures/synthetic/conversion_km_by_risk_tertile.png

    Kaplan-Meier cumulative conversion for participants grouped by tertile of out-of-fold risk from the best model. Separation between the curves is what a C-index summarises; the high-risk third converts several times more often than the low-risk third over four years.

    figures/synthetic/conversion_calibration_36m.png

    Predicted vs observed (Kaplan-Meier) probability of conversion by 36 months, by fifth of predicted risk. Points on the diagonal mean the predicted probabilities can be read as probabilities, not only as rankings.

    figures/synthetic/trajectories_MMSE.png

    Mean MMSE trajectory with 95% confidence band from a linear mixed model (random intercept and slope per participant), converters vs stable MCI. The synthetic cohort was generated with a 1.5 points/year faster decline in converters; the model estimates 1.15/year, lower because "converter" is defined from observed follow-up while the simulated decline uses the latent conversion time.

    figures/synthetic/trajectories_ADAS13.png

    Same model for ADAS-Cog 13 (higher is worse): converters worsen about 1.8 points/year faster.

    Resting-state EEG (OpenNeuro ds004504, real data)

    figures/open/eeg_band_power_by_group.png

    Relative spectral power per band, averaged over the 19 channels, one point per participant. The known slowing of the EEG in Alzheimer's disease is visible as more theta and less alpha in AD than in controls; FTD sits in between. Delta dominates every group because these preprocessed recordings carry most of their power below 2 Hz (see limitations in RESULTS.md).

    figures/open/eeg_topomap_theta_alpha.png

    Scalp maps of the log theta/alpha power ratio per group. Controls keep a posterior alpha rhythm (blue, low ratio); in AD the ratio is high over the whole scalp; in FTD the posterior alpha is partly preserved.

    figures/open/eeg_roc_AD_vs_CN.png

    Participant-level ROC curves for AD vs controls (logistic regression and LightGBM on spectral features; each participant's prediction is the mean over their epochs, averaged over five repeats of subject-level 5-fold CV). AUC about 0.90.

    figures/open/eeg_roc_FTD_vs_CN.png

    Same for FTD vs controls: AUC 0.74 (logistic) to 0.83 (LightGBM), with wide intervals (23 vs 29 participants).

    figures/open/eeg_roc_AD_vs_FTD.png

    AD vs FTD: close to chance (AUC 0.53-0.61). Spectral power separates dementia from health better than it separates the two dementias, which matches the subject-level literature.

    Mendelian randomisation (open summary statistics, real data)

    figures/open/mr_volcano.png

    Every circulating protein (UKB-PPP cis-pQTL) and NMR metabolite tested against Alzheimer's disease (Bellenguez et al. 2022): MR estimate vs significance. Large points pass FDR < 0.05 within their family. 44 proteins and 7 metabolites pass.

    figures/open/mr_top_forest.png

    The 20 most significant MR estimates with 95% confidence intervals (log-odds of AD per SD of the exposure). Most top hits sit in known AD GWAS loci (CR1, TREM2, CD2AP, EPHA1, ACE, GRN, CD33); with a single cis instrument and no colocalisation, a hit means "the protein is a candidate", not "the protein is causal".

    Sensitivity analyses (phase 2)

    figures/open/eeg_band_power_by_group_delta1.png

    Same as the band-power figure, with the delta band and the total power computed from 1 Hz instead of 0.5 Hz. Delta still holds about 80% of the power in every group, so the dominance of slow activity in these recordings does not depend on where the band starts.

    figures/open/eeg_topomap_theta_alpha_delta1.png

    Theta/alpha scalp maps with the 1-Hz lower edge. Identical pattern: posterior alpha in controls, diffuse slowing in AD.

    figures/open/eeg_roc_AD_vs_CN_delta1.png

    AD vs controls with the 1-Hz lower edge: AUC 0.90 (logistic and LightGBM), as in the primary analysis.

    figures/open/eeg_roc_FTD_vs_CN_delta1.png

    FTD vs controls with the 1-Hz lower edge: AUC 0.76 (logistic) and 0.83 (LightGBM), as in the primary analysis.

    figures/open/eeg_roc_AD_vs_FTD_delta1.png

    AD vs FTD with the 1-Hz lower edge: still near chance.

    figures/open/mr_bellenguez_vs_kunkle.png

    MR estimate of every protein and metabolite with the primary AD GWAS (Bellenguez 2022, which includes UK Biobank, x axis) against an AD GWAS without UK Biobank (Kunkle 2019, y axis). Orange points are the 50 molecules selected for ADNI: 42 of the 45 testable ones keep their sign. The estimates for all traits correlate at r = 0.42, since many of them are pure noise; the selected ones sit along the diagonal.

    figures/open/mr_volcano_kunkle.png

    Volcano plot of the screen with Kunkle 2019 as outcome. Fewer traits pass FDR (9 proteins) because the GWAS is smaller, and because without allele frequencies every palindromic instrument has to be dropped.

    figures/open/mr_top_forest_kunkle.png

    The 20 strongest estimates with Kunkle 2019 as outcome. The nine at FDR < 0.05 are CR1, EPHA1, CELA2A, PILRA, PILRB, C1S, C1R, CD33 and IL34. All but CELA2A are among the molecules selected in the primary analysis.

    figures/open/eeg_roc_AD_vs_CN_eegnet.png

    Participant-level ROC of EEGNet for AD vs controls: AUC 0.81 (0.69-0.90), below the 0.91 of the spectral logistic model on the same folds.

    figures/open/eeg_roc_FTD_vs_CN_eegnet.png

    EEGNet for FTD vs controls: AUC 0.64 (0.48-0.78). With 52 participants the network overfits.

    ADNI (aggregate results)

    figures/adni_public/conversion_km_by_risk_tertile.png

    ADNI, 1,146 participants with MCI. Kaplan-Meier cumulative conversion by tertile of out-of-fold predicted risk (gradient-boosted survival). The high-risk third converts in most cases within five years, the low-risk third rarely. Curves stop where fewer than 10 people remain at risk.

    figures/adni_public/conversion_calibration_36m.png

    Predicted vs observed conversion by 36 months, by fifth of predicted risk (about 230 people each). The points lie on the diagonal.

    figures/adni_public/conversion_cindex_forest.png

    C-index of the four models on ADNI. The clinical baseline reaches 0.69; the three full models reach 0.83 and are indistinguishable from each other.

    figures/adni_public/trajectories_MMSE.png

    Mixed-model mean MMSE trajectory for converters vs stable MCI on ADNI: 1.27 points/year faster decline in converters.

    figures/adni_public/trajectories_ADAS13.png

    Same for ADAS13: 2.85 points/year faster worsening in converters.

    figures/adni_public/trajectories_CDRSB.png

    Same for CDR-SB: 1.07 points/year faster worsening in converters.

    figures/adni_public/omics_proteins_qq.png

    QQ plot of p-values from Cox models of MCI-to-dementia conversion, one per CSF aptamer (SomaScan 7k; 389 participants, 170 conversions). The whole proteome departs from the diagonal because CSF proteins track ongoing neurodegeneration (top: 14-3-3 proteins, NEFH). The 48 aptamers of the MR candidates (orange) follow the same curve as the rest: no enrichment.

    figures/adni_public/omics_proteins_selected_forest.png

    Hazard ratio of conversion per SD for each aptamer of the MR-selected proteins, adjusted for age, sex and APOE4. ACE stands out (HR 0.76, p = 0.0006), in the direction predicted by MR. Most intervals include 1.

    figures/adni_public/omics_metabolites_qq.png

    The same QQ plot for 250 serum NMR measures (786 participants, 342 conversions): mild departure from the diagonal, no measure at FDR < 0.05, and the 6 MR-selected metabolites near the diagonal.

    figures/adni_public/fusion_cindex.png

    C-index (out-of-fold, 95% bootstrap CI) for each combination of data blocks, with ridge Cox and gradient-boosted survival, on all 1,146 ADNI participants with MCI. Adding the clinical scales lifts the baseline from 0.69 to 0.82. Each biomarker block adds at most about 0.01, and all blocks together about 0.02. Biomarkers without CDR-SB and FAQ reach 0.78-0.79.

    figures/open/mr_coloc_candidates.png

    Posterior probabilities from coloc ABF for the nine strongest MR candidates against AD, using UKB-PPP regional statistics (±500 kb) and Bellenguez 2022. Green: shared causal variant (H4). Red: distinct variants in the same region (H3). Seven proteins colocalise; ACE and EPHA1 do not under the single-variant model.
